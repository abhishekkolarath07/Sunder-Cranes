Safety

Zero room for assumption.

Every lift is engineered before a machine leaves the yard. Six gates, each signed off and documented — the sequence does not move forward until the previous stage closes.

  1. 01

    Planning

    Site survey, access route study, ground conditions, obstruction mapping, method statement issued for review.

  2. 02

    Load analysis

    Weight verification, centre of gravity, sling angles, dynamic factors, ground bearing pressure calculation.

  3. 03

    Equipment selection

    Load chart matching at radius, configuration and counterweight selection, utilisation capped below rated limit.

  4. 04

    Rigging

    Tackle inspection and certification, spreader configuration, trial lift, toolbox talk with the full crew.

  5. 05

    Execution

    Exclusion zone enforced, single appointed signaller, wind monitoring, controlled hoist and slew to position.

  6. 06

    Handover

    Position verification, de-rigging, site demobilisation, complete lift documentation issued to the client.

About

Since 1984. Four decades of moving what others cannot.

From a single crane serving regional contractors to a multi-class fleet working nationwide.

  1. 1984

    Founded

    Established as a single-crane operation serving regional industrial contractors.

  2. 1996

    Heavy class

    First 100 T class machines introduced; move into refinery and power sector work.

  3. 2008

    Engineering cell

    In-house lift engineering and method statements become standard on every project.

  4. 2019

    Crawler fleet

  5. Today

    Pan-India
